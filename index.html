Why are your prices so affordable compared to big agencies?

Because you're not paying for account managers, boardroom meetings, or a fancy office. We're a lean studio with a proven build process designed specifically for local service businesses, so our time goes into your design and your copy—not into reinventing the basics. You get a professional, fast website without the agency markup. Any third-party costs, like registering a new domain name, are explained upfront before you pay anything.

How does the free preview work?

Send us your business name and what you offer on WhatsApp. We design a working preview of your website and send you a link you can open on your own phone. If you love it, we refine it and launch. If it's not for you, you walk away—no payment, no obligation, no awkward follow-ups.

What do you need from me to get started?

Just the basics: your business name, the services you offer, the areas you work in, and the number customers should WhatsApp or call. If you have a logo, photos of your work, or prices you'd like shown, send those too. Don't have a logo or great photos yet? No problem—we'll design around what you've got.

How fast will my website be live?

Starter sites can be live within 48 hours. Most Growth and Custom Pro builds launch within 2 to 7 days. The biggest factor is how quickly you send feedback and content—the faster you reply on WhatsApp, the faster you're live and taking enquiries.

Case Study • Live Client Build

Swiss Spit Braai

How we turned a catering menu into a quote-request machine that works on any phone.

The Brief

Catering sells on appetite and trust. People planning weddings, birthdays and year-end functions want to see the food, understand what each package includes, and get a price fast—almost always from their phone. The site needed to do all three without a single dead end.

Design Decisions

  • Warm, ember-toned palette

    Rich fire-and-smoke colours and big food imagery sell the experience in the very first screen.

  • Packages as tap-friendly cards

    Each menu package shows what's included at a glance, so customers choose before they message—fewer back-and-forth questions.

  • Thumb-first layout

    Large type, high contrast and big buttons that stay readable outdoors and easy to tap one-handed.

Layout Structure

  1. 01Hero + instant quote button
  2. 02Menu packages
  3. 03Events we cater
  4. 04Mobile-first gallery
  5. 05How booking works
  6. 06WhatsApp quote & contact
